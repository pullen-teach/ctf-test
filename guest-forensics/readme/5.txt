Mission 5: Log detective   [Medium, 200 points]

Objective: Find the IP address behind the break-in, then its one successful login.

  cd ~/mission5
  head auth.log

auth.log records every login attempt to a server. Someone guessed
passwords over and over, then got in.

Count the Failed attempts per IP address. The busiest IP is the
attacker. Its Accepted line holds the flag.

Useful commands: grep, cut, sort, uniq
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 5   (2 hints, 10 points each: 5% of this mission)
