Mission 6: Hidden tail   [Medium, 200 points]

Objective: Find the message hidden after the end of a picture (steganography).

  cd ~/mission6
  filetype cat.jpg

cat.jpg opens as a normal picture. But picture viewers stop at the
image's end marker, and anything stored after it stays invisible.

Hiding data inside another file is called steganography. Here somebody
appended a message to the end of the picture, then encoded it. Look at
the readable text near the end.

Useful commands: strings, tail, base64
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 6   (2 hints, 10 points each: 5% of this mission)
