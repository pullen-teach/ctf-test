Mission 9: Leaked in pieces   [Hard, 400 points]

Objective: Rebuild data that left the network in small pieces, from a packet capture.

  cd ~/mission9
  head -5 capture.txt

capture.txt is a packet capture, listed the way Wireshark shows it: one
line per packet, with its time, source and destination addresses,
protocol and a summary.

One host kept requesting /pixel.gif with extra values: a seq number and
a chunk of hex data. Put the chunks in seq order, join them and decode
the hex.

Useful commands: grep, cut, sort, tr, xxd
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 9   (3 hints, 20 points each: 5% of this mission)
