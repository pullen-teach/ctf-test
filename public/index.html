Nothing to install

Everything runs on your own device, inside this tab. Works on Chromebooks.

Don't reload mid-game

A reload builds a fresh computer: new flags, zero points, a new clock.

Warm up first

New to CTFs? The Warm-ups tile is optional and untimed. Each CTF's clock starts when you begin Mission 1.

Stuck?

Every command has --help. Hints cost 5% of a mission's points.
