How to play
  1. Every forensics tool explains itself: strings --help, filetype --help, tar --help.
  2. Click the terminal and type the commands. Copy puts a command on your clipboard to paste.
  3. Paste with Ctrl+V or a right-click. Highlighting text copies it; middle-click pastes it.
  4. Prefer the terminal? Your home folder and each mission folder have a README.txt: cat README.txt (or mission 1).
  5. A flag looks like CYBA{word-1a2b3c4d}. Check it: submit CYBA{...}
  6. Stuck? hint 1 (up to 10). A hint costs 5% of that mission's points, charged once.

Flags change every time this page loads.
