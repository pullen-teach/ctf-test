Mission 9: The vault   [Very Hard, 800 points]

Objective: Recover the keyword, then decode and decrypt the vault.

  cd ~/mission9
  cat README.txt

The final mission chains everything. vault.b64 was encrypted with a
Vigenère cipher, then encoded in base64.

The keyword is written in key.txt, but that note was itself scrambled
with a Caesar shift. Recover the keyword, decode the vault, decrypt it.

Useful commands: caesar, base64, vigenere
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 9   (4 hints, 40 points each: 5% of this mission)
