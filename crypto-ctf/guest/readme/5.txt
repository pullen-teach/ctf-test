Mission 5: Russian doll   [Medium, 200 points]

Objective: Peel three layers of encoding, one at a time.

  cd ~/mission5
  cat doll.txt

Like a set of Russian dolls, this message is wrapped in three layers:
hex, base64 and ROT13. Working out the order is part of the puzzle.

Look at the text, decide which layer is on the outside, decode it, then
look again. Pipes (|) chain the steps into one command.

Useful commands: xxd, base64, caesar
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 5   (2 hints, 10 points each: 5% of this mission)
