CyberQuest Warm-ups
===================

Two quick warm-ups to get you ready for a CTF. Both are optional, both
are untimed, and the hints are free.

  1  Orientation     how a CTF works; submit your first flag   50 pts
  2  Speed drills    Tab, up arrow, Ctrl+C, copy and paste      50 pts

Read one with:  mission 1   (or mission 2)
Start here:     cat orientation.txt

When you're ready, pick a CTF from the home page.
