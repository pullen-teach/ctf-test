Warm-up 1: Orientation   [50 points, untimed]

Objective: Read the CTF orientation and submit your first flag.

  cat orientation.txt

orientation.txt in your home folder explains how a CTF works, what a
flag looks like, how scoring works and the rules. Read it with cat.

The warm-up flag is on the last line. Submit it:
  submit CYBA{...}

When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
