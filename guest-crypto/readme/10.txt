Mission 10: The vault   [Very Hard, 800 points]

Objective: Crack the keyword's hash, then open the vault.

  cd ~/mission10
  cat README.txt

The final vault combines three skills. It was encrypted with a Vigenère
keyword, then base64-encoded. Nobody wrote the keyword down: only its
SHA-256 fingerprint survived.

Crack the fingerprint against the wordlist, then use the keyword to open
the vault.

Useful commands: hashlines, grep, base64, vigenere
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 10   (4 hints, 40 points each: 5% of this mission)
