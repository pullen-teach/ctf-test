Mission 2: Hex marks the spot   [Easy, 100 points]

Objective: Turn hexadecimal back into readable text.

  cd ~/mission2
  cat secret.hex

secret.hex is a long run of digits and the letters a to f. That is
hexadecimal: every two characters are one byte, one letter of the
message.

xxd is a hex tool. It turns bytes into hex, and hex back into bytes.

Useful command: xxd
When you have the flag: submit CYBA{...}
Stuck? hint 2   (1 hint, 5 points each: 5% of this mission)
